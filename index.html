ETHOS

02 — THE DISCIPLINE

"I don't build tools. I build leverage."
02.1

The Pragmatic Idealist

In Kalobeyei and Kakuma, digital constraints are not limitations; they are structural boundaries. I engineer web solutions built to perform on unstable connections, low-bandwidth, and minimal hardware. Technology must solve the human equation where it is, not where we pretend it to be.

02.2

Radical Simplicity

Complexity is easy. Simplicity is a discipline. By stripping away unnecessary dependencies, proprietary frameworks, and recurring software subscriptions, I build highly resilient digital solutions that organizations completely own from day one.

02.3

Education as Infrastructure

Software is only as capable as the people operating it. Every deployment I build is accompanied by targeted community workshops, turning digital intimidation into functional competence for local youth and growing organizations.
